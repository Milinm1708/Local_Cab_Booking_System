Our story

Local rides, built on open technology.

LocalRide started as a final-year engineering project with one goal: prove that a genuinely useful, Uber-style booking platform doesn't need paid map APIs or expensive infrastructure. Every pin you drop uses OpenStreetMap data, every route is drawn with Leaflet.js, and the whole backend runs on Django with a single SQLite file.

Today the platform connects everyday riders with verified local drivers across mini, sedan and SUV categories — with transparent, formula-based fares and a real admin workflow for approving drivers and monitoring the marketplace.
